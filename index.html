WELCOME TO BANANA TREE DAYCARE

A second home where every child feels seen & inspired.

At Banana Tree Daycare in Calgary, we believe early childhood should be filled with wonder, laughter, and genuine discovery.

Inspired by modern early-learning best practices, our classrooms are thoughtfully organized into pretend-play kitchens, reading corners, sensory stations, and collaborative art tables—giving children the freedom to explore at their own pace.

✓ Warm, attentive & qualified educators
✓ Clean, sunlit & secure learning rooms
✓ Social, emotional & school-readiness focus
✓ Daily outdoor active play & fresh air

THE BANANA TREE DIFFERENCE

Designed around how children truly learn.

Every corner of our centre balances joyful imagination with safety, structure, and warmth.

01

Imaginative Pretend-Play Worlds

From full-size play kitchens and multi-story dollhouses to community helper stations, children build empathy, storytelling, and social skills through role-play.

02

Creative Arts & Sensory Exploration

Painting, building blocks, tactile sensory bins, and music spark curiosity and fine-motor confidence every single morning.

03

Private Outdoor Play Yard

Our securely fenced outdoor playground features colorful playhouses, shaded gazebo seating, climbers, and lush green lawn.

04

Cozy Reading & Early Literacy Corners

Accessible bookshelves, alphabet & number displays, and daily story circles nurture a lifelong love of books and communication.
